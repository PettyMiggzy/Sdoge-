Marketplace: list your NFTs for sale.

Buy and sell SDOGE Collectibles, Community Art and creators' own SDOGE Studio collections. A 2% fee on each sale goes to $SDOGE stakers' USDC rewards, and creators get their royalty. Listing moves your NFT into the marketplace contract (escrow) until it sells or you cancel, so every listing you see can really be bought.
