Launch your own collection.

Your own ERC-721 contract, owned by your wallet. Mint in batches, airdrop to your holders, or open a public drop at your price. Every token uses one credit. Royalties (up to 10%) are paid on resales at the SDOGE marketplace. The supply cap can only ever go down.

How a drop works

1. Pin your metadata folder (1.json, 2.json, ...) and save its base URI. 2. Save the drop terms: price, per-wallet limit and optional start and end times (a free drop needs a supply cap). 3. Open the drop, check the saved terms it shows you, and share its link. Collectors pay your price, which goes to you. Each mint uses one of your credits, which all your collections share; anyone can add credits to your wallet, so running out only stops the drop until more are added. Close the drop when you're done. If some tokens are rarer than others, point the base URI at placeholder metadata until the drop sells out, then reveal (and freeze).
