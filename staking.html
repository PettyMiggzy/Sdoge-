FAQ

Staking, explained.

Before your stake matures (80% of its lock), leaving costs a 15% principal penalty (fixed in the contract) plus 100% of that stake's unclaimed reward. The forfeited reward goes back into the pool for everyone else, and the SDOGE penalty goes to the Treasury to be turned into rewards. The site shows the exact cost and asks you to confirm before an early exit. After 80%, none of this applies.

Yes. Each stake is tracked separately with its own tier, unlock time, and reward — stake into different tiers, or the same tier multiple times, whenever you want.

From SDOGE NFT profits: once the marketplace and the Studio are connected to the pool, the marketplace fee on every resale and a share of every Studio mint-credit sale flow in on their own (the NFT and Studio pages show where fees go right now), and the team adds USDC from NFT sales. Rewards forfeited by early exits go back in, and anyone can top up the pool. The size of the pool depends on NFT volume, so it isn't guaranteed. The Treasury schedules reward periods, and once the first one has run, if a week passes without a new one, anyone can restart it from what's waiting in the pool.

Yes, with the contract's withdraw function (for example from Arc's explorer); this page exits whole stakes. From a matured stake it costs nothing. From a stake that hasn't matured, taking any part out forfeits that stake's whole accrued reward and charges the penalty on the part you take, and only if you pass allowEarly = true; otherwise it reverts. Each stake is its own position, so your other stakes aren't touched.
