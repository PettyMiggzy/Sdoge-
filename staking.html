Frequently asked questions

Connect your wallet on Arc, enter an amount, pick a lock period and press Stake. Your wallet first asks you to approve that exact amount of SDOGE, then to stake it. To add an NFT boost, pick one of your SDOGE NFTs before you stake.

Rewards build up every second, in $SDOGE and USDC. Once your stake matures (at 80% of its lock) you can claim them and keep the stake running, or take them when you unstake.

Yes. Each stake has its own lock: 7, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, earning 1.0×, 1.2×, 1.5×, 2.0× or 3.0× the rewards. A stake counts as matured at 80% of its lock, so a 30-day stake is penalty-free after 24 days. These terms are fixed in the contract: nobody can change them.

Yes. After your stake matures it costs nothing. Before that, leaving costs 15% of what you take out, and that stake's unclaimed rewards are forfeited. None of it leaves the pool: it goes to the stakers who stay. The page shows you the exact cost and asks you to confirm first. You can also send what you unstake to up to 4 wallets, split any way you like, with "Send to other wallets".

$SDOGE rewards come from the team's dev fees, from early-exit penalties and from forfeited rewards. USDC rewards come from SDOGE NFT profits: the marketplace fee, a share of Studio sales and NFT sales. How much there is depends on that activity, so rewards aren't guaranteed.

Pick one of your SDOGE NFTs when you stake and that stake's share of the rewards goes up, by up to 50% depending on the design (the page shows the boost when you pick it). The NFT is held with the stake and comes back to you when the stake ends, early or not. It's never penalized.
